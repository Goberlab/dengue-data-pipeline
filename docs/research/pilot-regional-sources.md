# Pilot regional dengue sources

## Short answer

Piura is the only pilot region with public, machine-readable, case-level surveillance files on datosabiertos.gob.pe, and the broader Piura vector-borne file (`dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv`, 188,876 dengue rows across 2023 to mid-2026) strictly supersedes the defective Piura dengue-only file (`dataset_dengue_actualizado.csv`, which drops 2024, shifts its 2023 date columns, and mixes non-dengue and discarded rows into its 2026 cut).
The Piura metaxenicas file includes onset date (`FECHA_INI`), notification date (`FECHA_NOT`), NOTIWEB system entry date (`FECHA_ING`), and case class (`TIPO_DX`), so the Piura onset-to-notification delay EDA (issue #12) can proceed immediately.
Neither Tumbes nor Lambayeque publishes regional dengue CSV or Excel case files on datosabiertos.gob.pe or their DIRESA/GERESA portals; both publish PDF epidemiological reports sourced from MINSA's NOTIWEB system (with Lambayeque's weekly dengue PDF containing a district-by-epi-week matrix, and Tumbes's PDFs containing district cumulative plus current-epi-week counts), and neither exposes case-level notification dates.
Whether `UBIGEO` in Piura's open-data CSVs records place of infection or patient residence is UNCONFIRMED because the two official DIRESA Piura data dictionaries contradict each other.

## Piura

### What exists

Gobierno Regional de Piura (authored by DIRESA Piura, Dirección de Inteligencia Sanitaria, UF Epidemiología) publishes two case-level CSV files containing dengue cases on Peru's national open-data portal, plus weekly epidemiological PDFs and a Power BI dashboard on its gob.pe portal. A complete scan of all 32 catalog entries under the GORE Piura group ([datosabiertos.gob.pe GORE Piura group](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/group/gobierno-regional-de-piura-gore-piura)) confirmed no other regional dengue or arbovirus files exist there.

### Fields

#### Metaxenicas CSV (`dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv`)

UTF-8 (with BOM), comma-delimited, 19 columns, 0 null values across all 190,686 rows:
`FECHA_CORTE`, `DEPARTAMENTO`, `PROVINCIA`, `DISTRITO`, `UBIGEO`, `ANO`, `DIAGNOSTIC`, `N_DIAGNOSTICO`, `TIPO_DX`, `EDAD`, `TIPO_EDAD`, `SEXO`, `FECHA_INI`, `FECHA_NOT`, `E_SALUD`, `NOMBRE_ESTABLECIMIENTO`, `SEMANA_NOT`, `FECHA_ING`, `TIPO_NOTI`.

Note one header mismatch: both the CKAN description and [Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx) name the eighth column `NOMBRE_DIAGNOSTIC`, whereas the CSV header is `N_DIAGNOSTICO`.

Key field definitions from [Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx) and empirical verification on the downloaded CSV:
- `DIAGNOSTIC` / `N_DIAGNOSTICO`: ICD-10 disease code and label. Dengue codes are `A97.0` (167,396 rows), `A97.1` (20,828 rows), and `A97.2` (652 rows), totaling 188,876 dengue rows. The remaining 1,810 rows are other vector-borne or related conditions (`B55.1` cutaneous leishmaniasis 1,252; `A92.0` chikungunya 465; `U06.9` Zika 30; `G61.0` Guillain-Barre 30; `B55.2` mucocutaneous leishmaniasis 11; `B51` P. vivax malaria 9; `A44.0`/`A44.1`/`A44.9` Carrion's disease 8; `A92.5` severe chikungunya 2; `B57` Chagas 2; `B50` P. falciparum malaria 1).
- `TIPO_DX`: case class (`C` confirmed, `P` probable, `D` discarded, `S` suspected). Among the 188,876 dengue rows: 116,450 `C`, 1,965 `P` (118,415 confirmed or probable), 70,461 `D`, and 0 `S` (the 21 `S` rows are non-dengue).
- `FECHA_INI`: symptom onset date (`YYYYMMDD`).
- `FECHA_NOT`: notification date (`YYYYMMDD`). In all 188,876 dengue rows, `FECHA_NOT >= FECHA_INI` (0 negative gaps).
- `SEMANA_NOT`: notification week number. It matches the MINSA/CDC epi-week of `FECHA_NOT` on all 190,686 rows (100.0%).
- `FECHA_ING`: date the record was entered into the surveillance system (`YYYYMMDD`). Across all 188,876 dengue rows, 461 rows have `FECHA_ING < FECHA_INI` (down to -361 days) and 1,072 rows have `FECHA_ING < FECHA_NOT`, consistent with manual date-entry errors.
- `E_SALUD` / `NOMBRE_ESTABLECIMIENTO`: 10-character notifying facility code (whose first 6 digits are a district UBIGEO) and facility name.
- `TIPO_NOTI`: surveillance mode (`P` passive: 189,364 rows; `A` active: 1,320 rows; `E` mass events: 2 rows).
- `UBIGEO` / `DEPARTAMENTO` / `PROVINCIA` / `DISTRITO`: **Place basis is UNCONFIRMED.** [Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Formato_DiccionarioDatos_metaxenicas.xlsx) defines `DEPARTAMENTO`, `PROVINCIA`, and `DISTRITO` as where the diagnosed person resides ("donde reside la persona diagnosticada con enfermedad metaxénica"), whereas DIRESA Piura's companion dengue dictionary ([Formato_DiccionarioDatos_DENGUE_0.xlsx](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Formato_DiccionarioDatos_DENGUE_0.xlsx)) defines them as where the case occurred ("donde sucedió el caso") and mistakenly describes `UBIGEO` as the location of a hydrological station ("estación hidrológica"). Empirically, among the 188,876 dengue rows, 185,994 have both `UBIGEO` and `E_SALUD[:6]` in Piura (`20xxxx`), 2,131 have `UBIGEO` in Piura with notifying facilities outside Piura (led by Lima `15xxxx` with 962, Lambayeque `14xxxx` with 228, La Libertad `13xxxx` with 226, Callao `07xxxx` with 215, and Tumbes `24xxxx` with 153), 750 have `UBIGEO` outside Piura (led by Lambayeque with 250, Tumbes with 175, and Lima with 104) with notifying facilities inside Piura, and 1 has both outside Piura. Seventy-three dengue rows with single-digit department codes (`01` to `07`) lost their leading zero and appear as 5-digit `UBIGEO` strings. Exact duplicate excess rows total 1,582 across the full file (1,581 in dengue rows; 1,356 in confirmed or probable dengue rows).

#### Dengue-only CSV (`dataset_dengue_actualizado.csv`)

UTF-8 (with BOM), comma-delimited, 15 columns, 47,082 rows (118 excess exact duplicate rows; 1,612 null `LOCALIDAD` values; 41 five-digit `UBIGEO` strings missing a leading zero):
`FECHA_CORTE`, `DEPARTAMENTO`, `PROVINCIA`, `DISTRITO`, `UBIGEO`, `FECHA_INGRESO`, `ANO`, `LOCALIDAD`, `CENTRO_SALUD`, `EDAD`, `TIPO_EDAD`, `SEXO`, `FECHA_INICIO_SINTOMAS`, `FECHA_NOTIFICACION`, `SEMANA_NOTIFICACION`.

It omits `DIAGNOSTIC`, `N_DIAGNOSTICO`, `TIPO_DX`, `E_SALUD`, and `TIPO_NOTI`. Its dictionary ([Formato_DiccionarioDatos_DENGUE_0.xlsx](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Formato_DiccionarioDatos_DENGUE_0.xlsx)) defines `FECHA_NOTIFICACION` as the date the patient attended the health facility ("Fecha en que el paciente acudió al centro de salud") and `FECHA_INGRESO` as the date of entry into the information system.

Cross-matching `dataset_dengue_actualizado.csv` against `dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv` uncovered two severe structural defects:
1. **Cyclically shifted date columns in the 2023 cut (`FECHA_CORTE=20230517`, 24,566 rows):** `FECHA_INGRESO` is always earlier than or equal to `FECHA_INICIO_SINTOMAS` (23,645 strictly earlier, 921 equal, 0 later) and `SEMANA_NOTIFICACION` equals the epi-week of `FECHA_INICIO_SINTOMAS` on all 24,566 rows. Matching `(UBIGEO, EDAD, TIPO_EDAD, SEXO, FECHA_INGRESO, FECHA_INICIO_SINTOMAS, FECHA_NOTIFICACION)` in `dataset_dengue_actualizado.csv` to `(UBIGEO, EDAD, TIPO_EDAD, SEXO, FECHA_INI, FECHA_NOT, FECHA_ING)` in `dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv` matches 23,802 of 24,566 rows (96.9%), proving that the 2023 cut shifted `FECHA_INI -> FECHA_INGRESO`, `FECHA_NOT -> FECHA_INICIO_SINTOMAS`, and `FECHA_ING -> FECHA_NOTIFICACION`, and that it mixes confirmed, probable, and discarded cases (only 18,534 of 23,988 onset-matched rows are confirmed or probable).
2. **Unfiltered metaxenicas and discarded cases in the 2026 cut (`FECHA_CORTE=20260702`, 22,516 rows):** All 22,516 rows match the 22,516 rows of `dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv`'s `20260701` cut on 9 shared fields (100.0%). Contrary to the catalog notes describing confirmed and probable dengue cases, this cut contains 440 non-dengue rows (409 cutaneous leishmaniasis, 10 Zika, 8 chikungunya, 5 malaria, 4 mucocutaneous leishmaniasis, 4 Carrion's disease) and 15,659 discarded dengue rows (`TIPO_DX=D`), leaving only 6,417 confirmed or probable dengue rows with no columns to separate them.

- `FECHA_CORTE=20260701` (22,516 total rows; 22,076 dengue; 440 non-dengue):
  - `ANO=2025`: 11,926 dengue rows (1,161 `C`, 1 `P`, 10,764 `D`); `FECHA_INI` `2024-12-29` to `2026-01-03`; `FECHA_NOT` `2024-12-29` to `2026-01-13`; `FECHA_ING` `2024-04-07` to `2026-03-09`.
  - `ANO=2026`: 10,150 dengue rows (3,369 `C`, 1,886 `P`, 4,895 `D`); `FECHA_INI` `2026-01-04` to `2026-06-29`; `FECHA_NOT` `2026-01-04` to `2026-06-30`; `FECHA_ING` `2026-01-05` to `2026-06-30`. Probable cases in 2026 are heavily concentrated near the cut date (1,243 of 1,886 `P` rows have onset in June 2026), reflecting pending laboratory classification at the July 2026 snapshot.

Preliminary delay check on confirmed and probable dengue rows (`C+P`) in `dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv` confirms the file supports issue #12 across epidemic and inter-epidemic years:
- `FECHA_NOT - FECHA_INI` (onset to notification): median 3 days in every year (2023 to 2026); 90th percentile 7 days in 2023, 9 days in 2024, 7 days in 2025, and 6 days in 2026 (87.9% to 95.0% within 7 days).
- `FECHA_ING - FECHA_INI` (onset to NOTIWEB system entry, closer to operational `available_at`): median 6 days in 2023, 5 in 2024, 6 in 2025, and 4 in 2026; 90th percentile 78 days in 2023, 41 days in 2024, 23 days in 2025, and 9 days in 2026 (only 56.5% entered within 7 days in 2023 and 65.5% in 2024, showing substantial epidemic-year data-entry backlogs).

### Access and update cadence

- **WAF / User-Agent requirement:** On `datosabiertos.gob.pe`, default `curl` User-Agent requests return `HTTP 418` (Huawei Cloud WAF, `Server: CW`). Sending a standard desktop Chrome `User-Agent` returns `HTTP 200` for catalog HTML, CKAN `package_show` JSON, and direct file downloads.
- **Broken portal APIs:** `https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/api/3/action/package_search?q=dengue&rows=50` returns `HTTP 301` / `HTTP 404`, and the DKAN datastore endpoint (`https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/api/action/datastore/search.json?resource_id=570f8e2b-6f55-4ecd-b8a0-13c47e3201af`) returns `HTTP 500`.
- **License:** Both packages carry the Open Data Commons Attribution License ([odc-by](http://opendefinition.org/licenses/odc-by/)).
- **Real update cadence:** Both metadata sheets declare "Monthly" updates, but actual updates are sporadic (roughly annual).
  - The metaxenicas package ([node/20815/revisions](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/node/20815/revisions)) was created `2025-06-03`, edited in July and August 2025, and updated once more on `2026-07-02` (`Last-Modified: Thu, 02 Jul 2026 16:29:29 GMT`); no updates occurred between July 2026 and November 2026.
  - The dengue-only package ([node/12780/revisions](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/node/12780/revisions)) was created `2023-05-18`, edited in August 2023, updated with new data on `2026-07-02` (`Last-Modified: Thu, 02 Jul 2026 17:48:08 GMT`), and received metadata-only edits on `2026-09-14` and `2026-09-15`.
  - Individual revision view URLs on `datosabiertos.gob.pe` (such as [node/12780/revisions/37500/view](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/node/12780/revisions/37500/view) and [node/20815/revisions/49656/view](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/node/20815/revisions/49656/view)) return `HTTP 403 Forbidden`, and Wayback Machine CDX queries ([dengue CSV CDX](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/dataset_dengue_actualizado.csv&output=json), [metaxenicas CSV CDX](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/dataset_metaxenicas_saneada020726.csv&output=json)) return `[]`, so historical snapshots are not publicly retrievable.
- Neither Piura open-data CSV is updated weekly, so they cannot feed live weekly epi-weeks of the panel; they serve historical analysis and the delay EDA (issue #12).

## Tumbes

### What exists

Tumbes publishes **no** regional dengue CSV, Excel, or case-level open data.
