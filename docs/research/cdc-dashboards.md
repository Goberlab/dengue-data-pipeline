# CDC Perú dengue dashboards

Investigation of all dengue-related dashboards and endpoints on `app7.dge.gob.pe` (and their official entry points on `www.dge.gob.pe`), verified against live HTTP responses, Shiny Server WebSocket sessions, downloaded workbooks, Internet Archive CDX queries, and the historical case file on 2026-11-22 / 2026-11-23 UTC.

## Short answer

Yes: two live Shiny apps on `https://app7.dge.gob.pe/maps2/` expose a national **district by epi-week** dengue series, while `https://app7.dge.gob.pe/maps2/sala_diaria_dengue.html` does not ([issue #3 comment](https://github.com/Goberlab/dengue-data-pipeline/issues/3#issuecomment-7089262174)).
1. **Observatorio de Clima y Salud** (`https://app7.dge.gob.pe/maps2/shiny_observatorio_web/`) serves bulk department `.xlsx` downloads (`clima_departamento.xlsx`) containing every district with its 6-digit `UBIGEO` by epi-week from `2018-SE01` to `2026-SE45`, filterable by case class (`CONFIRMADO`, `PROBABLE`, `TOTAL`). Across all 65 Piura districts (`2018-2025`), it matches the historical onset-week case file on `99.20%` of cells (`26,952 / 27,170`, `-0.417%` total deficit), but has a Lima Metropolitana double-counting bug in its `NACIONAL` rollup and zero-pads future epi-weeks after `SE 45`.
2. **Sala Situacional de Enfermedades Metaxénicas** (`https://app7.dge.gob.pe/maps2/shiny_metaxenicas_web/`, target of `sala_metaxenica`) exposes an exact `100.00%` match to the historical onset-week case file (`261 / 261` epi-weeks for both confirmed and probable in Piura `200101`, `2021-SE01` to `2025-SE53`) inside its interactive `ten-fig02a-echart` chart, but requires one Shiny query per district by name without `UBIGEO` and its case `.xlsx` downloads have no district by epi-week series.
3. **Mapa de Calor de Dengue** (`https://app7.dge.gob.pe/maps2/shiny_dengue_heatmap_web/`) only covers a rolling 4.5-month window (`2026-07-01` to `2026-11-14`) for autochthonous cases with no downloads; **Distritos Fronterizos** (`shiny_fronteras_web/`) crashed on init (`code 4503`); **Wolbachia** (`shiny_wolbachia_web/`) covers only 4 Lima districts; **Febriles** (`shiny_febriles_web/`) carries no dengue data.
4. No dashboard explicitly documents onset week vs notification week in UI text (proven empirically here against the historical file), and Internet Archive captures of Shiny apps save only empty UI shells with zero surveillance data, so our own weekly snapshot archive is mandatory for `available_at`.

### Key reconciliation findings on Observatorio de Clima y Salud

Verified locally against `datos_abiertos_vigilancia_dengue_2000_2025_0.csv` (the historical onset-week national case file) using `obs_piura_dep_total.xlsx` (`30,550` rows, all 65 Piura districts) and `obs_nacional_total.xlsx`:

- **All 65 Piura districts x 418 epi-weeks (2018 to 2025, 27,170 cells)**: `26,952 / 27,170` cells (`99.20%`) match the historical case file identically. `2018` (`525` vs `525`), `2020` (`125` vs `125`), and `2025` (`1,117` vs `1,117`) are `100.0%` exact on every district and epi-week. Across all 8 years, `0` cells have `Observatorio > Historical`; `218` cells (`0.80%`) have `Observatorio < Historical`, totaling `-540` cases out of `129,610` (`-0.417%`), concentrated in `2023` (`78,846` vs `79,304`, `-458`) and `2024` (`32,195` vs `32,246`, `-51`). The exact exclusion rule causing that `-0.4%` historical deficit in Observatorio is **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Piura district (`200101`) in 2026 (`SE 1` to `SE 45`)**: Observatorio gives `1,016` total (`511` confirmed, `505` probable; `SE 44 = 187`, `SE 45 = 126`), matching `shiny_metaxenicas_web` on `45 / 45` epi-weeks (`100.0%`). Across all 10 districts of Piura province in 2026 (`SE 1` to `SE 45`), Observatorio (updated `18-11-2026`) totals `9,571` vs `9,587` in `shiny_metaxenicas_web` (updated `20-11-2026`, `-16` cases across 6 districts), and across all of Piura department `19,234` vs `19,263` (`-29` cases), reflecting the 2-day update lag and/or the `-0.4%` filter difference.
- **Confirmed `NACIONAL` double-count bug**: Observatorio's `Pais` sheet reports `283,814` in 2023 (historical `256,641`, surplus `+27,173`; Lima Metropolitana historical = `28,272`), `344,198` in 2024 (historical `271,531`, surplus `+72,667`; Lima Metropolitana = `74,040`), `36,986` in 2025 (historical `35,853`, surplus `+1,133`; Lima Metropolitana = `1,204`), and `121,867` in 2026 `SE 1-45` (`shiny_metaxenicas_web` = `112,610`, surplus `+9,257`). This occurs because `tendencias-sel_depa` includes both `LIMA` and `LIMA METROPOLITANA` and the `Pais` table sums both.

## Proposed pass/fail test for issue #15

Decision issue #15 (`https://github.com/Goberlab/dengue-data-pipeline/issues/15`) needs a reproducible gate before selecting a live district by epi-week case source (primary candidate: `shiny_observatorio_web` bulk department `.xlsx` downloads; secondary/reference candidate: `shiny_metaxenicas_web` `ten-fig02a-echart`).

### Test panel and sentinel districts

Run the test on all three pilot departments (`PIURA`, `TUMBES`, `LAMBAYEQUE`), with at least six sentinel districts spanning high-burden urban and rural districts plus one cross-province duplicate name check:

- Piura: `200101` (`PIURA / PIURA / PIURA`), `200104` (`PIURA / PIURA / CASTILLA`), `200114` (`PIURA / PIURA / TAMBO GRANDE`).
- Lambayeque: `140101` (`LAMBAYEQUE / CHICLAYO / CHICLAYO`), `140308` (`LAMBAYEQUE / LAMBAYEQUE / OLMOS`).
- Tumbes: `240101` (`TUMBES / TUMBES / TUMBES`).

For any name-keyed source (`shiny_metaxenicas_web`, `sala_diaria_dengue.html`), normalize and join strictly on `(departamento, provincia, distrito)` via the official district reference geography crosswalk (issue #7), never on bare district name.

### Pass/fail gates

1. **Gate 1, two-snapshot repeatable retrieval (`available_at` replay check)**:
   Capture two successive weekly snapshots `S_t` (e.g. cut `SE 45`, `available_at` = UTC fetch time after publication) and `S_{t+1}` (cut `SE 46`, one epi-week later) from the same candidate source for all pilot districts.
   - **Pass**: Both snapshots succeed programmatically without manual clicks; the UI-reported cut epi-week advances by `+1`; all rows with `epi-week > cut_epi_week` are masked as missing (never stored as `0`); closed historical epi-weeks (`2018-2025`) are bit-for-bit identical between `S_t` and `S_{t+1}`; and recent open epi-weeks (`cut - 4` to `cut`) show coherent revision behavior (revisions stored as a new snapshot row stamped with `available_at`, not overwritten).
   - **Fail**: Either snapshot fails to connect/download, schema changes silently, cut epi-week cannot be parsed to mask zero-padded future rows, or historical years mutate unexpectedly.
2. **Gate 2, historical onset-week reconciliation (2021-2025 overlap)**:
   Join snapshot `S_t` against `datos_abiertos_vigilancia_dengue_2000_2025.csv` on `(UBIGEO, epi-year, onset week)` for all districts in the three pilot departments across `2021-SE01` to `2025-SE53`, separately for `TOTAL`, `CONFIRMADO`, and `PROBABLE`.
   - **Pass**:
     - Zero systematic lag shift: lag-0 correlation beats lag `+1` and lag `-1` on every sentinel district, and the fraction of cells with `source > historical` is `< 0.1%` (confirming onset week rather than notification week).
     - Exact cell match rate `>= 99.0%` across all pilot district by epi-week cells, and total absolute case discrepancy `<= 0.5%` per pilot department and per case class (for context, `shiny_observatorio_web` already achieves `99.20%` exact and `-0.417%` total discrepancy across all 65 Piura districts in 2018-2025; `shiny_metaxenicas_web` already achieves `100.00%` exact on `200101`).
   - **Fail**: Evidence of notification-week alignment (systematic forward shift), unresolved `UBIGEO` mismatches, or discrepancy `> 0.5%` without a documented definitional cause.
3. **Gate 3, live cross-source consistency (2026 overlap)**:
   For `2026-SE01` through the shared cut epi-week (e.g. `SE 45`), reconcile the primary live source (`shiny_observatorio_web`, aggregated from its `Distrito` sheets only, never its `Pais` sheet) against `shiny_metaxenicas_web` (`ten-fig02a-echart` on the 6 sentinel districts and `ten-tbl06a/c` on the 3 pilot departments) by case class (`CONFIRMADO`, `PROBABLE`).
   - **Pass**: Sentinel district counts match `shiny_metaxenicas_web` within reporting-lag tolerance when update dates differ (for the 2026-11-18 vs 2026-11-20 captures, Piura `200101` matched `45 / 45` epi-weeks `100%`, and Piura department matched within `-29 / 19,263` = `-0.15%`), and `CONFIRMADO + PROBABLE == TOTAL` holds on every district-epi-week row.
   - **Fail**: Divergent 2026 district counts beyond the update-date lag, broken `CONFIRMADO + PROBABLE = TOTAL` additivity, or Lima double-counting leaking into national sums.

### Failure fallback for issue #15

If `shiny_observatorio_web` fails Gate 1 (e.g., download handler breaks) or Gate 2 on Tumbes/Lambayeque, fall back to `shiny_metaxenicas_web` (`ten-fig02a-echart`) for the pilot districts (slower one-district-per-query Shiny extraction, no UBIGEO, exact `100%` match to historical). If both live Shiny apps fail Gate 1 or cease operating, live district by epi-week is unavailable from `app7.dge.gob.pe` (`sala_diaria_dengue.html` only supports department by epi-week + cumulative district totals), and issue #15 must fall back to regional pilot sources (issue #5), coarser department-level live series, or waiting on the MINSA transparency request (issue #21).

## Open questions

1. **Exact cause of Observatorio's `-0.417%` historical deficit (`-540 / 129,610` cases in Piura 2018-2025)**: Why does `shiny_observatorio_web` drop `458` Piura cases in 2023 and `51` in 2024 compared to both `datos_abiertos_vigilancia_dengue_2000_2025.csv` and `shiny_metaxenicas_web` (while matching `100.0%` in 2018, 2020, and 2025)? Candidate explanations to verify in Tumbes/Lambayeque or ask CDC Perú (issue #21): exclusion of imported cases (`autóctonos` only, matching `shiny_dengue_heatmap_web`), exclusion of records with missing onset date, or an unrefreshed 2023-2024 archive table.
2. **Tumbes and Lambayeque reconciliation in Observatorio**: Only Piura (`65` districts) was downloaded and reconciled in this ticket to keep within one session per app; Tumbes and Lambayeque should be run during the Gate 2 pass/fail execution in issue #15 (or issue #2 snapshot archive).
3. **Confirmed and probable separation in Observatorio bulk downloads**: In `obs_piura_dep_total.xlsx` (downloaded with `Tipo caso = TOTAL`), only `TOTAL` was exported; downloading `obs_piura_dist_prob.xlsx` (with `Tipo caso = PROBABLE`) returned `PROBABLE` only (`505` for Piura `200101` in 2026, matching `511 + 505 = 1,016`). Confirm during issue #2 whether two department downloads (`CONFIRMADO` and `PROBABLE`) or three (`+ TOTAL`) are needed per snapshot to preserve full case-class splits and additivity checks.
4. **Official confirmation of epi-week definition across dashboards**: None of `shiny_observatorio_web`, `shiny_metaxenicas_web`, or `sala_diaria_dengue.html` explicitly prints "fecha de inicio de síntomas" in its UI text (and `shiny_dengue_heatmap_web` even labels its count "casos notificados" despite matching onset-week counts). Empirical reconciliation proves onset week for `2018-2025`, but issue #21 (MINSA transparency request) should ask CDC Perú to confirm in writing whether live 2026 epi-weeks can temporarily fall back to notification week when onset date is missing.
5. **Broken `shiny_fronteras_web` backend and `ten-tbl08c-download` 404**: Both failed on CDC Perú's Shiny Server during 2026-11-23 UTC probes; low priority because `shiny_fronteras_web` only covers 84 border districts and `ten-tbl08c` only covers a single epi-week by notifying facility.
6. **Observatorio's weekly ERA5-Land / GPM IMERG climate columns (`tmean`, `tmax`, `tmin`, `humr`, `ptot`)**: Flag for issue #18 ("Which climate and ENSO sources can serve both history and live weeks for Peru?") that `shiny_observatorio_web` already publishes pre-aggregated district-by-epi-week climate series (`2018-SE01` to `2026-SE45`, with 1981-2020 normals and `n_dias` completeness count) alongside dengue in the exact same `UBIGEO`-keyed `.xlsx`, though its spatial aggregation method (plain vs population-weighted mean) is undocumented and needs verification before use.
